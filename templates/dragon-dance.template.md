# Dragon Dance

Use this only when `foresight` or the session revealed a real workflow lesson —
a pattern of drift, a verification gap, or a handoff failure that could repeat.

If nothing was learned, skip this entirely.

## Problem
- What caused friction, ambiguity, or rework?

## Impact
- What did it slow down, hide, or risk?

## Improvement
- What change to the process, checklist, or template would help?

## New Convention
- What new rule should future agents follow?
